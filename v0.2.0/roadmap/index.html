Roadmap

What's coming next

The next releases, in the order we plan to ship them. Each server release comes with app updates for the same features. Plans change as we learn, so this page follows the issues on GitHub.

Next · v0.2

Dependable at home

Matinee keeps working when the internet is down, your watch history survives a replaced or renamed file, a wrong match is easy to fix, and when playback stutters Matinee tells you why. More files play as they are, and HDR looks right when it has to be converted.

  • ServerTest the Umbrel App Store package and submit it
  • ServerTest the ZimaOS / CasaOS App Store entry and submit it
  • ServerTest the TrueNAS app and submit it to the community train
  • ServerTest the Unraid template
v0.3

Plays everything well

Subtitles with their full styling, timing and audio delay controls, Dolby Vision handled for each kind of screen, several versions of a film under one title, and a TV app where your libraries are one press away.

  • ServerShow several versions of a movie or episode as one title, with a version pickerTop priority
  • ServerSubtitles: per-user preferences and smarter defaults
  • ServerSubtitles: VobSub (.idx/.sub) and PGS (.sup) sidecar files, and Subs/ folders
  • ServerSubtitles: render ASS/SSA with full styling (anime)
  • ServerVerify hardware transcoding on NVIDIA (NVENC/NVDEC)
  • ServerVerify hardware transcoding on Intel (VAAPI and Quick Sync)
  • ServerVerify hardware transcoding on Macs (VideoToolbox)
  • ServerVerify hardware transcoding on AMD with Windows (AMF)
  • ServerVerify hardware transcoding on Raspberry Pi (V4L2)
  • ServerMake dialogue easier to hear: night mode and volume leveling for films and shows
  • ServerSubtitle look and timing: size, color, background, and a sync offset
  • ServerDolby Vision: keep it where the screen supports it, fall back cleanly where it doesn't
  • ServerAudio delay control in the web player, for lip sync
  • AppsSubtitle look and timing in the phone and TV apps
  • AppsTV: your libraries one press away, and choose where the app opens
  • AppsShow ASS/SSA subtitles with their styling in the phone and TV apps
  • AppsAudio delay control in the phone and TV apps, for lip sync
  • AppsSame features on every app: a feature table and a parity check before release
v0.4

Your library, your way

Better matching for unusual names, extras, local .nfo files, a Home screen you arrange yourself, sign-in with your own identity provider, and streaming limits for each person.

  • ServerRecognize multi-episode files (S01E01E02, S01E01-E02)
  • ServerRecognize date-based episodes for daily shows
  • ServerDocs: a guide page for choosing your language
  • ServerDocs: a "Settings at a glance" page
  • ServerRecognize episodes named without an S01E01 code
  • ServerPin a match with ids in folder names, and keep same-named shows apart
  • ServerShow extras (featurettes, deleted scenes, behind the scenes) and skip sample files
  • ServerRead local .nfo files and keep manual edits
  • ServerPlayback history for admins, and why each stream is being converted
  • ServerPer-person streaming limits: quality away from home and simultaneous streams
  • ServerSign in with your own identity provider (OpenID Connect)
  • ServerCustomize Home: reorder, hide and pin rows
  • ServerSync watched history and ratings with Trakt
  • ServerDocs: screenshots for the guide pages that have none
  • ServerDocs: show the phone and TV apps in the Watching pages
