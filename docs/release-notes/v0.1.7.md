Playlists, podcasts and voice control arrive, along with CarPlay, more album covers found on their own and a cover picker for the ones that aren't.

### New

- **Playlists**: make your own music playlists, add songs, albums or whole artists, reorder them and play them on the web, phones, TVs and in the car. Each person and profile keeps their own. See [Music](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/music/#playlists).
- **Podcasts**: a new Podcasts library. Add shows by their feed or search for them through Podcast Index (with a free key an admin saves in Settings → Podcast search), follow the ones you like, and find new episodes on Home and in Continue listening. Episodes play in the audiobook player, with show notes, on the web, phones and TVs. See [Podcasts](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/podcasts/).
- **Voice control**: "Hey Siri, play Abbey Road on Matinee", and the same with Google Assistant and Android Auto, for songs, albums, artists, playlists and audiobooks. See [Voice control](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/apps/voice/).
- **CarPlay**: your music and audiobooks in the car on an iPhone. See [CarPlay](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/apps/carplay/).
- **Pick a cover yourself**: admins can click an album's or book's cover to choose another one from the Cover Art Archive, Apple, Deezer or Open Library, or use their own image. See [Picking a cover yourself](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/music/#picking-a-cover-yourself).

### Better

- **Soundtracks and compilations stay on one album** instead of splitting into one album per artist, and their songs name each artist. Libraries that are already split are fixed on the next scan. See [Soundtracks and compilations](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/music/#soundtracks-and-compilations).
- **More album covers are found on their own**: from MusicBrainz ids in your tags, from titles with typos or notes like "(CD 2)", and with another try each month for albums still missing one.
- **Scrub through songs and audiobooks with the TV remote**. See [Music on a TV](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/music/#on-a-tv).
- With no signal in the car, CarPlay and Android Auto still show the lists you opened and play your downloads.

### Updating

Packages, the Docker image and the Helm chart update as usual. See [Updating](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/getting-started/updating/) in the guide. The apps have all of this from version 1.0.19.
