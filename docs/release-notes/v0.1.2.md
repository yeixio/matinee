Audiobooks arrive: add an Audiobooks library and listen in the web player and the phone and TV apps, with chapters, speed control and a sleep timer.

### New

- **Audiobooks** (optional): a new library kind next to Movies, TV and Music. Books are grouped by author, series and narrator, from their tags or from folders like `Author/Series/Book 2 - Title/`. A whole `.m4b` and books split into parts or CD folders both work, and covers, `desc.txt`, `reader.txt` and `author.jpg` are picked up.
- **A book player** that keeps playing while you browse, with the chapter list, previous and next chapter, back 15 seconds and ahead 30, speeds from 0.75× to 3× remembered per profile, and a sleep timer that can stop at the end of a chapter. Lock-screen and media keys skip back and ahead.
- **Continue listening** on Home, with your place kept across the whole book. A book counts as finished in its last minute.
- Book pages with resume, mark as finished and more in the series, plus author pages and Books, Authors, Series and Narrators tabs.
- The phone and TV apps play audiobooks in their next update.

See [Audiobooks](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/audiobooks/) and how to organize them in [Organizing your media](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/getting-started/organizing-media/).

### Updating

Packages, the Docker image and the Helm chart update as usual; database changes apply on start. See [Updating](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/getting-started/updating/) in the guide.
