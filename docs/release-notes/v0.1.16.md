Tune in to internet radio, browse your music by record label, and add big podcasts without a hitch.

### New

- **Radio stations**: music libraries get a **Radio** tab. Find stations in the free [Radio Browser](https://www.radio-browser.info/) directory by name or genre, or add one by its stream address, and play it live with the song that's on. Your server plays each station for you, so stations work from anywhere you reach Matinee. See [Radio stations](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/music/#radio-stations).
- **Record labels**: albums remember the record label their tags name, and a new **Labels** tab lists every label in a library with its albums. Album pages link to their label. See [Record labels](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/music/#record-labels).

### Better

- **Adding big podcasts**: a show with hundreds of episodes is no longer dropped when it takes a while to add, and admins see why a feed couldn't be read. See [Podcasts](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/podcasts/).

### Updating

Packages, the Docker image, the Helm chart, the Mac app and the Windows app update as usual. See [Updating](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/getting-started/updating/) in the guide. Albums already in your library get their record label on the next scan. The apps get radio stations and record labels from version 1.0.25.
