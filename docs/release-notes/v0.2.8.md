Matinee 0.2.8 turns your phone or tablet into a remote for Matinee on your TV.

### New

- **Your phone as the TV's remote**: in the phone and tablet apps, **Settings → Remote** picks a TV with Matinee open and drives it: arrows and OK (or swipe on the pad), Back, Home, play and pause, skip and stop, and typing straight into the TV's search with your phone's keyboard. It works with Apple TV, Android TV, Google TV, Fire TV, Samsung and LG TVs, and every press goes through your own server. See [Your phone as the TV's remote](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/apps/phone-remote/).
- At home, grown-ups now also see and control the TVs nobody signed in on (the shared Home account), as anyone at home could pick up that TV's remote anyway.

### Apps

Version 1.0.43 of the phone and TV apps adds the Remote on phones and tablets, and lets TVs take presses and typing from it.

### Updating

Packages, the Docker image, the Helm chart, the Mac app and the Windows app update as usual. See [Updating](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/getting-started/updating/) in the guide.
