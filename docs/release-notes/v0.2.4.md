Matinee 0.2.4 plays games: add your games as a library, pick one and play it with a controller, in the browser or on a phone or TV. It also matches tricky files better, starts videos in your language, keeps working while a network share is down, and lets Box Office fix a bad copy by itself.

### New

- **Games (preview)**: Matinee runs the game on the server and streams it to your screen, the way it streams a film, so there's nothing to install or set up where you play. Add a **Games** library and everyone you share it with gets box art and a **Play** button. Built in: Game Boy, Game Boy Color, Game Boy Advance, NES, SNES, Genesis/Mega Drive, N64 and PlayStation, and admins can add an emulator for another system. Up to four controllers play at once, and phones get buttons on the screen. Matinee plays only the games you already have; it never looks for or downloads games. Games come with the Docker image and the Helm chart for now, and stream over UDP port 7173. See [Games](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/server/games/).
- **Start in your language**: a video starts with the sound in your language, at the best quality, instead of whatever the file marks as its default, and remembers the tracks you pick for each film or show. See [Which sound and subtitles a video starts with](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/playing/#which-sound-and-subtitles-a-video-starts-with).
- **A to Z**: large libraries have the letters down the side, so you can jump straight to a title. See [Browsing](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/browsing/).
- **Search by cast**: find movies and shows by who's in them.
- **Box Office fixes problems**: **Report a problem** on a movie or episode, and with Box Office connected a bad copy gets replaced by itself. Requests go to Box Office's Requests page. See [Box Office](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/server/radarr-and-sonarr/#problems-box-office-fixes-by-itself).
- **Restart server** in Settings → Server health, and the logs of earlier runs and crashes kept for you. See [Server health](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/server/server-health/).

### Better matching

- **Ids in names**: put `{tmdb-603}` or `{imdb-tt0133093}` in a folder or file name to pin its match. Same-named shows stay apart.
- **Movies in several files** (CD1, CD2) play as one, and full-length Disk1/Disk2 copies become versions.
- **Episodes** in one file (S01E01E02), without S01E01, daily shows by date, and anime numbered from the first episode, in aired, DVD or absolute order.
- **.nfo files** are read, cast and crew included, and details you edit by hand are kept.

See [Organizing your media](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/getting-started/organizing-media/).

### More dependable

- **Network shares that stop answering** no longer hold up pages or background work. A title's page still loads and says why it won't play. See [Troubleshooting](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/help/troubleshooting/).
- **Background work pauses while something plays**, so playback has the disks and network to itself. See [Media analysis](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/server/media-analysis/#pause-while-something-plays).
- **Out of room to convert**: transcodes keep within the room they have, admins get an alert before a video fails, and players say so instead of a vague error. See [Transcoding](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/server/transcoding/#when-the-transcode-folder-runs-short-of-room).
- **Damaged files** are tested again before being called damaged, listed in Server health, and no longer retried over and over.
- **Sound in sync on iPhone, iPad and Apple TV** with 4K and HDR films played as they are, including after resuming.
- **Scans**: pick how often libraries are fully rescanned (every 6 hours by default) and how often network shares are checked, stop a running scan, and see what started it. See [New files](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/server/new-files/).
- **Time zone** in Settings. See [Time zone](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/server/configuration/#time-zone).
- **Helm**: upgrades no longer fail on volumes that can't grow. See [Kubernetes](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/server/kubernetes/#volume-sizes).

### Apps

Version 1.0.38 of the phone and TV apps plays games from your Games library on iPhone, iPad, Android, Android TV, Fire TV, Samsung and LG, with buttons on the screen or up to four controllers. Apple TV doesn't play games yet. Earlier app versions since 0.2.3 brought the A to Z bar, chapters and remote scrubbing on Apple TV, your language's tracks, and clearer reasons when a title won't start.

### Updating

Packages, the Docker image, the Helm chart, the Mac app and the Windows app update as usual. See [Updating](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/getting-started/updating/) in the guide. To play games with Docker, publish UDP port 7173 (`-p 7173:7173/udp`); the Helm chart opens it by itself.
