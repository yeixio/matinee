Adaptive streaming quality arrives, playback picks up after a server restart for music and audiobooks too, and the web player is easier to use on a phone. The apps add Android Auto and a Wear OS watch remote.

### New

- **Adaptive streaming quality** (optional): turn on **Adaptive quality** in Settings → Transcoding and players get a stream that steps between 1080p, 720p, 480p and 360p as the connection changes. Pick the steps you want, and set **Limit away from home** to cap the quality outside your home network. See [Streaming quality](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/server/streaming-quality/).
- **A quality menu in the web player**: Auto, a fixed step, or Original. Auto plays the file as it is when it can, and moves to the adaptive stream if playback keeps stalling. The phone and TV apps have the same picker from version 1.0.14.
- **Android Auto**: play your music and audiobooks in the car. See [Android Auto](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/apps/android-auto/).
- **Wear OS**: a watch remote for whatever Matinee is playing. See [Wear OS watches](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/apps/wear-os/).

### Better

- **Music and audiobooks pick up after a server restart or update**, at the same moment and speed, like videos already do.
- **The web player on a phone** has the same tabs as the phone app: Home, Libraries, Discover, Search and Settings. A new Libraries page reaches every library, plus your watch list, collections, Coming soon and requests.
- Audiobook views fit across a phone screen.
- The guide shows the audiobook player on a TV.

### Updating

Packages, the Docker image and the Helm chart update as usual. See [Updating](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/getting-started/updating/) in the guide.
