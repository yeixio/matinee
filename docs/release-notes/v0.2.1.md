Matinee 0.2.1 brings the first part of 0.3, "Plays everything well": subtitles that follow you, pick themselves and line up with the sound, several versions of a movie as one title, extras, and audio delay for lip sync.

### New

- **Subtitles that follow you**: choose when subtitles come on (never, forced lines only, when the sound isn't in your language, or always), in which languages, and how they look: size, color, edge, background and position. Your choices follow you to every app. See [Subtitles](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/playing/#subtitles).
- **Subtitles On picks for you**: the subtitles menu opens with **Off** and **On**, and On picks the best track in your languages. Every track, and Find subtitles, are under **More subtitles**.
- **Sync to audio**: Matinee lines subtitle files up with the sound on its own, and **Sync to audio** in the player does it for any subtitles in one press. See [Syncing subtitles to the audio](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/syncing-subtitles/).
- **Styled subtitles**: anime and fan-subtitled ASS/SSA subtitles keep their signs, positions, colors and the file's own fonts. See [Subtitles](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/playing/#subtitles).
- **Several versions of a movie**: a 4K copy and a 1080p one, or the theatrical and extended cuts, show as one title with a version for each. See [Versions](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/title-pages/#versions).
- **Extras and commentaries**: behind-the-scenes, deleted scenes, featurettes and commentary tracks show on a title's page. See [Extras and commentaries](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/extras/).
- **Audio delay**: when the sound comes before or after the lips move, for example with a soundbar or Bluetooth headphones, move it in steps of 25 milliseconds. See [Audio delay](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/playing/#audio-delay).

### Better

- **Subtitle timing**: move subtitles that come too early or too late, kept for each title.
- **Picture subtitle files**: Blu-ray `.sup` and DVD `.idx`/`.sub` files next to a video play, and subtitles in a `Subs` or `Subtitles` folder are found, including one folder per episode. See [Subtitles](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/getting-started/organizing-media/#subtitles).

### Apps

Version 1.0.27 of the phone and TV apps adds the same subtitle choices, styling, timing and Sync to audio, Off and On in the subtitles menu, versions, extras and audio delay.

### Updating

Packages, the Docker image, the Helm chart, the Mac app and the Windows app update as usual. Matinee backs up its database before this update. See [Updating](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/getting-started/updating/) in the guide.
