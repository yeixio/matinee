Matinee 0.2 is about being dependable at home: it keeps working when the internet is down, keeps your watch history when files change, backs itself up, and tells you why a video buffered. It also adds Live TV.

### New

- **Live TV**: an admin adds playlists of channels, such as iptv-org's lists of publicly streamed channels, and **Live TV** lists them with a guide of what's on now and next. Channels that stop working are hidden. See [Live TV](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/live-tv/).
- **Backups**: Matinee backs up its database every day and before each update, keeps the last week, and lets an admin download a backup or restore one from **Settings → Server health → Backups**. Admins get an alert if a backup fails. See [Backups](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/server/backups/).
- **Why it buffered**: when a video stalls, the player says why, such as a slow disk, a slow connection or a busy conversion, and what would help. The player menu shows the same, with a connection test. See [Why it buffered](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/why-it-buffered/).
- **Fix a wrong match**: search for the right title, pick it, refresh its details or unmatch it, right from its page. See [Fix a wrong match](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/getting-started/posters-and-details/#fix-a-wrong-match).
- **Mark as watched**: mark a movie, an episode, a whole season or a whole show as watched or unwatched from its page. See [Mark as watched](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/title-pages/#mark-as-watched).
- **HDR on screens without it**: when an HDR video is converted for a screen that can't show HDR, its colors are tone mapped so it doesn't look washed out. See [HDR on screens without it](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/server/transcoding/#hdr-on-screens-without-it).

### Better

- **No internet, no problem**: your server keeps playing and scanning at full speed when the internet is down, says calmly where a feature needs it, and catches up when it's back. See [When the internet is down](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/server/no-internet/).
- **Replaced and renamed files**: upgrading a file, renaming it or moving it keeps its watch history, ratings and place to resume from. See [Upgraded and renamed files](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/server/new-files/#upgraded-and-renamed-files).
- **HEVC and AV1**: devices that play HEVC or AV1 get the video repackaged instead of converted, which is faster and keeps full quality. See [Repackaging instead of converting](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/server/transcoding/#repackaging-instead-of-converting).
- **Server health** shows streams that are stalling.

### Apps

Version 1.0.26 of the phone and TV apps adds Live TV and the buffering explanation with a connection test in Settings, keeps working at home when the internet is down, and asks each device what it can play instead of guessing.

### Updating

Packages, the Docker image, the Helm chart, the Mac app and the Windows app update as usual. Matinee backs up its database before this update. See [Updating](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/getting-started/updating/) in the guide.
