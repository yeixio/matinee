Music gets a visualizer, and albums and audiobooks find their covers far more often.

### New

- **A music visualizer** in the web player: open it from the full player for bars, a halo round the cover, or rings that pulse with the beat. The arrow keys change the look, **Space** pauses and **Esc** closes it. The server works out each song's sound levels once, so the Apple TV app can show the visualizer too.
- **Find artwork** in Settings → Libraries for music and audiobook libraries looks up pictures again for every artist, album, author and book that has none.
- **Open Library** covers for books without their own, and photos for authors. It needs no key and is on by default.

### Better

- **More folder covers are found**: names in any case (`Folder.JPG`, `Cover.png`), `.jpeg` and `.webp`, `front` and `album` images, or a folder's only image. Back covers, disc scans and booklets are skipped.
- Albums split into `CD 1` and `Disc 2` folders use the cover in the album folder, and are no longer filed as an album called "CD 1".
- A cover added after the first scan is now picked up on the next full scan.
- Online album covers are found for more editions, such as "(Deluxe Edition)" or "[2011 Remaster]", and dead cover links are replaced.
- Compilations without an album artist stay together under Various Artists.
- A book's own description is kept when an online match has none.

See [Music](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/music/) and [Posters and details](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/getting-started/posters-and-details/) in the guide.

### Updating

Packages, the Docker image and the Helm chart update as usual. See [Updating](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/getting-started/updating/) in the guide. Press **Find artwork** after updating to fill in covers that were missing.
