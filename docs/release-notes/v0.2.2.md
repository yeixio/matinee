Matinee 0.2.2 continues "Plays everything well": Clear voice for dialogue you can hear, Dolby Vision that plays the way each screen takes it, posters without a TMDB key, and fixes for music and profiles on Apple TV.

### New

- **Clear voice**: when the dialogue is too quiet and the explosions too loud, turn on **Clear voice** in the player's sound menu. The server brings the voices forward and evens out loud and quiet moments. See [Clear voice](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/playing/#clear-voice).
- **Dolby Vision, the way each screen takes it**: Dolby Vision files play as Dolby Vision on screens that show it, and as HDR10 or HLG on the rest, instead of coming out with the wrong colors. See [Dolby Vision](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/server/transcoding/#dolby-vision).
- **Posters without a TMDB key**: with Radarr or Sonarr connected, Matinee gets posters, backdrops, descriptions, ratings and genres from them. See [Without a key: through Radarr and Sonarr](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/getting-started/posters-and-details/#without-a-key-through-radarr-and-sonarr).

### Better

- **Profile PINs on TVs**: a TV asks "Who's watching?" each time it opens, and now asks for a locked profile's PIN every time, even when that profile was the last one watching. See [PINs](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/household/profiles/#pins).

### Apps

Version 1.0.28 of the phone and TV apps adds Clear voice and Dolby Vision for each screen. On Apple TV, music shows in Control Center's Now Playing, keeps going to the next song after you leave the app, and has a **Stop** button, and locked profiles always ask for their PIN.

### Updating

Packages, the Docker image, the Helm chart, the Mac app and the Windows app update as usual. See [Updating](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/getting-started/updating/) in the guide.
