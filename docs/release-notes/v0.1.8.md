Matinee now runs on a Mac or a Windows PC with nothing to set up: download the app, open it, and it walks you through adding your media. Podcasts and playlists also reach the car and the watch.

### New

- **Matinee Server for Mac**: a menu-bar app that runs the server, signed and notarized by Apple. It walks you through creating your account and adding folders or network shares, can open at login and keep the Mac awake, and updates itself. See [Mac app](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/getting-started/mac/).
- **Matinee for Windows**: an installer for Windows 10 and 11 (x64 and Arm) with an icon in the notification area to start, stop and open the server. It starts at sign-in and offers updates as they come out. If Windows says it protected your PC, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. See [Windows](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/getting-started/windows/).
- **Podcasts in the car**: a Podcasts tab in Android Auto and CarPlay. See [Android Auto](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/apps/android-auto/) and [CarPlay](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/apps/carplay/).
- **Playlists and podcasts on the watch**: your playlists, new episodes and followed shows on Wear OS and Apple Watch, to play or download. See [Apple Watch](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/apps/apple-watch/) and [Wear OS watches](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/apps/wear-os/).

### Better

- The folder picker takes a typed folder or network share, such as `\\nas\media`, and lists the drives on Windows.
- A podcast episode played offline on a watch counts as finished in its last minute, like everywhere else.

### Updating

Packages, the Docker image and the Helm chart update as usual. See [Updating](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/getting-started/updating/) in the guide. The apps have the car and watch changes from version 1.0.20.
