Watch together with friends, skip TV intros, find subtitles online, and choose what quality to download on your phone.

### New

- **Friends and Watch together**: invite a friend with a single-use link and pick which libraries they see. Then start Watch together in the player and share the code, so everyone's playback stays in step on the web, phones and TVs. See [Friends](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/household/friends/) and [Watch together](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/watch-together/).
- **Skip intro**: Matinee finds TV intros by itself, from chapters or by matching the audio across a season's episodes, and players show **Skip intro** while one plays. See [Skipping intros](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/skipping-intros/).
- **Find subtitles**: when a film or episode has none in your language, **Find subtitles…** in the player's subtitles menu searches OpenSubtitles.com and adds the one you pick, for everyone. An admin adds a free OpenSubtitles key under Settings → Content → Subtitle search. See [Finding subtitles](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/finding-subtitles/).
- **Cars with Android built in**: Matinee for cars with Google built in, signed in from the car itself, with no phone needed. See [Cars with Android built in](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/apps/android-automotive/).

### Better

- **Downloads on phones**: pick a quality for each download, see a badge on what's downloaded, and filter the Downloads tab. See [Picking a quality](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/apps/downloads/#picking-a-quality).

### Updating

Packages, the Docker image, the Helm chart, the Mac app and the Windows app update as usual. See [Updating](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/getting-started/updating/) in the guide. After updating, the media analysis looks for intros in the background, one season at a time. The apps have all of this from version 1.0.22.

If Matinee helps you, you can [support it](https://yeix.io/matinee/donate).
