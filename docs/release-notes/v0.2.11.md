Matinee 0.2.11 brings your PlayStation and Xbox to every Matinee app, plays Steam on a PC you already have, and lets one library hold several folders.

### New

- **Xbox remote play (preview)**: an admin signs in to Xbox once, with a code at microsoft.com/link, and the account's consoles that have remote features on can be played from Games like any other game. See [Xbox remote play](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/server/games/#xbox-remote-play-preview).
- **PlayStation Remote Play works again**: 0.2.10's builds left the PlayStation bridge out by mistake. It's back, and a build without it now fails instead of shipping. See [PlayStation Remote Play](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/server/games/#playstation-remote-play-preview).
- **Steam on a PC you already have (preview)**: the Steam button can play Steam's Big Picture on a gaming PC running Sunshine. Pair once from Games; Matinee shows a PIN to type into Sunshine. Admins see the Steam setup at the top of a Games library until Steam is set up.
- **Several folders in one library**: in Settings → Libraries, the pencil on a library renames it and adds or takes out folders, so a second disk joins the library it belongs to. Two libraries of the same kind can be combined into one.
- **Leaving a game on purpose**: hold Select and Start together for 2 seconds to leave, with a small bar while you hold. A quick press of both still reaches the game.
- **Box Office**: Discover shows where to watch a title and Box Office's "Because you have" rows, Home gets a Leaving soon row of titles Box Office is about to clear out, and Matinee tells Box Office what has been watched (counts only, never who) so its clean-up rules can use it.

### Fixed

- Arcade games play at their real speed, so their sound isn't choppy.
- Game box art shows again, now that libretro's thumbnail site stopped serving pictures.
- Shows numbered by year, such as S2009E03, find their episodes' details.
- In the library editor, a quick second click no longer takes out the folder you just added.
- The web player keeps working after a Home guest signs in, and a title that's gone says so instead of spinning.

### Apps

Version 1.0.50 of the phone and TV apps:

- Paired PlayStations and the Xbox account's consoles are at the top of each Games library, for admins, on iPhone, iPad, Android, Apple TV, Android TV, Fire TV, and Samsung and LG TVs.
- Hold Select and Start for 2 seconds to leave a game. Back, the home button and the × leave on a second press, so a stray tap doesn't end your game.
- A typed home address such as `nas` or `192.168.1.50` connects without needing `http://` or the port.
- TV focus no longer slips when moving up through rows.

### Updating

Packages, the Docker image, the Helm chart, the Mac app and the Windows app update as usual. See [Updating](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/getting-started/updating/) in the guide.
