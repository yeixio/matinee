Matinee comes to the Apple Watch, and both watch apps can now keep music and audiobooks on the watch for listening without your phone.

### New

- **Apple Watch**: a watch remote for whatever Matinee is playing, with a complication for your watch face. Your iPhone signs the watch in. See [Apple Watch](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/apps/apple-watch/).
- **Listening on the watch**: download albums and audiobooks to an Apple Watch or a Wear OS watch and play them through Bluetooth headphones, with no phone nearby. See [Apple Watch](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/apps/apple-watch/) and [Wear OS watches](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/apps/wear-os/). The apps have both from version 1.0.15.

### Better

- **Offline audiobook listening keeps your place**: when a phone or watch syncs listening it did offline, the server moves the whole book along, so Continue listening picks up where you left off on any device.
- The watch apps are translated into all 32 languages.

### Updating

Packages, the Docker image and the Helm chart update as usual. See [Updating](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/getting-started/updating/) in the guide.
