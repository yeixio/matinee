Rate what you watch and listen to, bring your history over from another media server, and browse podcasts by category. Settings is easier to find your way around too.

### New

- **Star ratings**: give movies, shows, episodes, albums, songs, books and podcasts 1 to 5 stars, half stars included, and sort by **My rating**. Each person keeps their own. On the web, phones and TVs. See [Your ratings](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/title-pages/#your-ratings).
- **Switching from another media server**: Settings → Import from another media server brings over each person's watched marks, places to resume from, ratings, collections and the posters they picked. Files are matched by path, people are matched to Matinee accounts or profiles, and the other server's files are never changed. See [Switching from another server](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/getting-started/switching/).
- **Browse podcasts by category**: Add shows has a row of categories, from Comedy to True crime, each listing what's trending in it. See [Browsing by category](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/podcasts/#browsing-by-category).

### Better

- **Settings is grouped** into You, Libraries, People and profiles, Content, Streaming, Network and Add-ons, instead of one long page.
- A poster picked by hand now stays through rescans on movies too, and **Use the automatic poster** brings back the automatic one.

### Updating

Packages, the Docker image, the Helm chart, the Mac app and the Windows app update as usual. See [Updating](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/getting-started/updating/) in the guide. The apps have ratings and podcast categories from version 1.0.21.
