Sing along, skip the credits, hear live albums without a break, and sign in a TV by pointing your phone at it.

### New

- **Sign in a TV with a QR code**: the Apple TV, Android TV, Samsung and LG apps show a QR code next to the sign-in code. Scan it with your phone to approve the TV. A TV that has never seen your server, such as one at a friend's home, can be set up from the Matinee phone app by scanning its QR code, with no address to type. The phone and TV talk only to each other and to your server. See [Signing in](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/apps/signing-in/).
- **Synced lyrics**: the music player shows a song's words, with the line being sung lit up. Click a line to play from there. Matinee reads lyrics from `.lrc` or `.txt` files next to your songs and from their tags, and an admin can turn on looking them up on LRCLIB, a free and open lyrics database. See [Lyrics](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/music/#lyrics).
- **Skip credits**: Matinee finds where the end credits start in episodes and movies. Up next shows as the credits begin, a **Skip credits** button shows when nothing comes next, and reaching the credits counts as watched. Admins can turn it off under Media analysis. See [Skipping credits](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/skipping-credits/).
- **Albums without gaps**: live albums and DJ mixes whose songs run into each other play without a break, and anyone in the household can turn **Play without gaps** on or off for an album. See [Albums without gaps](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/music/#albums-without-gaps).

### Better

- **Podcasts**: a podcast library opens on Search. Phones can download a show's newest episodes by themselves, keeping the last 1 to 10. When an episode ends the next one plays (Settings → Playback → Keep playing podcasts), and a show's episodes can be listed oldest first. See [Podcasts](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/podcasts/).

### Updating

Packages, the Docker image, the Helm chart, the Mac app and the Windows app update as usual. See [Updating](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/getting-started/updating/) in the guide. After updating, Matinee looks for end credits and gapless albums in the background, which can take a while on a big library. The apps get QR sign-in, lyrics, Skip credits, gapless albums and podcast downloads from version 1.0.24.
