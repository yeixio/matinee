Matinee 0.2.3 brings updates you can install from Settings, Box Office and Air-Mixer working alongside Matinee, friends who set up their phones and TVs without typing an address, and chapters on the seek bar.

### New

- **Updates from Settings**: Matinee looks for new versions by itself and says so in Settings. Where it can, it installs them too, automatically or when you press **Update**, and waits until nobody is watching. That covers apt, dnf and Homebrew installs, and Kubernetes with Argo CD. See [Updates](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/server/updates/).
- **Box Office**: connect Box Office in Settings beside Radarr and Sonarr, and Matinee fills in its address when it's on your home network. Requests go through it, Discover shows its recommendations when Overseerr isn't connected, and its news reaches people in the apps. See [Box Office](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/server/radarr-and-sonarr/#box-office).
- **Notifications on phones and tablets**: Box Office's news arrives as your phone's own notifications, even with Matinee closed, straight from your own server. The same one again is counted instead of stacked. See [Notifications](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/apps/notifications/).
- **Air-Mixer**: play your music on every speaker in the house through Air-Mixer, and use a TV, phone or browser with Matinee open as one of its speakers. See [Air-Mixer](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/server/air-mixer/).
- **Friends' phones and TVs without typing**: a friend's welcome link opens the app with your server already added. See [Phones and TVs without typing](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/household/friends/#phones-and-tvs-without-typing).
- **Chapters on the seek bar**: movies and episodes with their own chapters show a notch where each one starts, and the chapter's name above the preview while you scrub. A jump that has to restart a conversion keeps a picture on screen with a spinner instead of going black. See [Seek bar previews](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/seek-previews/#chapters-on-the-bar).

### Better

- **Recently added for each kind**: Home has its own Recently added row for movies, shows, music and the rest, newest first. See [Home](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/home/).
- **Soundtracks and compilations** show each song's artist. See [Soundtracks and compilations](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/music/#soundtracks-and-compilations).
- **Disk alerts** tell you about a full disk once, then only at each 5 points fuller, instead of over and over. See [Alerts](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/server/alerts/).
- **Radarr and Sonarr** are easy to disconnect from Settings.
- **The web music player** can be closed on phones and from the full player.
- **Stats for dashboards**: `/stats` gives a short summary as JSON for a dashboard such as Homepage. See [Dashboards](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/server/alerts/#dashboards).

### Apps

Version 1.0.32 of the phone and TV apps adds notifications from Box Office, welcome links that add a friend's server, the Recently added rows, song artists on soundtracks and compilations, and chapters on the seek bar. Samsung and LG TVs find the server on the home network, play music and show alerts, and a TV's sign-in code only needs approving once.

### Updating

Packages, the Docker image, the Helm chart, the Mac app and the Windows app update as usual. See [Updating](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/getting-started/updating/) in the guide. After the update, Matinee reads each file's description once more in the background to pick up its chapters. It only looks at each file's header, so it's quick.
