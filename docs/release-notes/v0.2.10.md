Matinee 0.2.10 fixes the web player on a plain home address, tightens security, and previews playing your PlayStation from Matinee.

### New

- **PlayStation Remote Play (preview)**: an admin can sign in to PlayStation once, pair a PS5 or PS4 on the home network, and play it from Games in the web player like any other game. It stays on your home network. This is an early preview. See [PlayStation Remote Play](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/server/games/#playstation-remote-play-preview).

### Fixed

- The web player no longer shows a blank page when you open Matinee by its plain `http://` home address, such as `http://192.168.1.50:7171`, in a browser that hasn't used it before. Watch together had the same problem and works again too.
- Sign-in and permission hardening.

### Apps

Version 1.0.47 of the phone and TV apps:

- OK on the phone or tablet remote now presses what's focused on Apple TV.

### Updating

Packages, the Docker image, the Helm chart, the Mac app and the Windows app update as usual. See [Updating](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/getting-started/updating/) in the guide.
