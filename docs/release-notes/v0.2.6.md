Matinee 0.2.6 finds box art for more games, lets you pick games by system, and brings a first preview of PC games from Steam.

### New

- **Box art for more games**: Matinee now uses pictures next to your games and in the folders game front ends and scrapers keep them in. Online, it picks the closest match even when a name differs a little, such as another region or a missing "The", and falls back to title screens and screenshots. See [Box art](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/server/games/#box-art).
- **Pick games by system**: in a Games library, chips at the top and a Platform section in Filters show one system's games, in the web app and the apps from 1.0.41. See [Picking a system](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/server/games/#picking-a-system).
- **PC games from Steam (preview)**: a game host runs beside Steam on a Linux computer with a graphics card. Matinee streams its games like console games. Admins can play Steam's Big Picture or single games from **Games** in the web app, and sign in to Steam through **Steam desktop**. See [PC games from Steam](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/server/games/#pc-games-from-steam-preview).

### Apps

Version 1.0.41 of the phone and TV apps adds the system chips to Games libraries. On TVs, Back now goes up to the top of a long page, and to the banner on Home.

### Updating

Packages, the Docker image, the Helm chart, the Mac app and the Windows app update as usual. See [Updating](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/getting-started/updating/) in the guide.
