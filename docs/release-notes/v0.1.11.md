See where you're going when you scrub, keep an eye on your server from one page, and keep TV shows out of movie rows.

### New

- **Seek bar previews**: hover over or drag the seek bar and a small picture of that moment shows above it, on the web, phones and TVs. Matinee makes the pictures in the background after the media analysis, gently, and pauses while anyone is transcoding. See [Seek bar previews](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/seek-previews/).
- **Server health**: a new Settings → Server health page for admins, with CPU, memory and bandwidth, what's playing now (with a Stop button), problems from the log, background jobs, free space on every disk, and a searchable log you can download. See [Server health](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/server/server-health/).

### Better

- **Movie rows are movies only**: Home's genre and holiday rows no longer mix in TV shows. Smart collections get an **Include TV shows** box; new ones are movies only unless it's ticked, and existing ones keep what they had. See [Collections](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/watching/collections/).

### Updating

Packages, the Docker image, the Helm chart, the Mac app and the Windows app update as usual. See [Updating](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/getting-started/updating/) in the guide. After updating, seek bar previews are made in the background, which can take a while on a big library. The apps show them from version 1.0.23.
