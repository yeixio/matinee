Matinee 0.2.5 brings games to Apple TV.

### New

- **Games on Apple TV**: from app version 1.0.40, Apple TV plays games from your Games library, with up to four controllers paired in its Bluetooth settings. Apple TV gets the game straight over the connection it already has to your server, so it doesn't need the games port. See [On Apple TV](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/server/games/#on-apple-tv).

### Apps

Version 1.0.40 of the phone and TV apps plays games on Apple TV.

### Updating

Packages, the Docker image, the Helm chart, the Mac app and the Windows app update as usual. See [Updating](https://matinee.yeix.io/docs/getting-started/updating/) in the guide.
