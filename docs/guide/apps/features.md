Every Matinee app plays from the same server, so your watch list, ratings, place in a film and settings follow you between them. Most features work everywhere. This page shows where each one is, so you know what to expect on the screen in front of you.

- **✓** The app has it. Pick a feature's name for how it works.
- **Planned** It's on the way. A link takes you to its issue on GitHub, where a 👍 tells us you want it.
- **–** The app doesn't need it, for the reason in the notes under the table.

Server settings, such as adding libraries, people and Live TV channels, are in the web player on any device: Settings in a browser signed in as an admin.
